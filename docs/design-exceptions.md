# Design exceptions

The one list of written exceptions to the Design and layout rules in CLAUDE.md. A rule in CLAUDE.md holds everywhere except the rows below. Each row keeps exactly the behaviour it had when it was a sentence in CLAUDE.md; moving it here changed nothing in the app.

Cap: 20 rows. A new exception needs Shubham's approval, a reason and a date; past 20, one must go first. Guard: `src/__tests__/design-exceptions.test.ts`.

| Part or screen                                                       | Exception                                                                                                                         | Why                                                                                                 | Date approved |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------- |
| Thread view (chat)                                                   | The ROOT message text and its strip label sit on the 16 gutter; replies keep their text column.                                   | Slack way: the root reads as the thread's heading, not as one more reply.                           | 9 Nov 2026    |
| VoiceNote (chat)                                                     | Voice note numbers are Inter (font-sans), not the mono tabular meta face.                                                         | They sit inside the message as part of the note, not as a date line; guarded in VoiceNote.test.tsx. | 7 Nov 2026    |
| Post screen, platform Preview                                        | Each platform is drawn in its own colours (no logos), outside the grey + amber/green/red token set.                               | The preview must look like the app the post goes to, or it does not preview anything.               | 7 Nov 2026    |
| Personal notes composer                                              | The hint stays "Note" (not the chat composer's hint).                                                                             | The notes are private to one person; "Note" says what the box is for.                               | 9 Nov 2026    |
| ui/UploadRing                                                        | X on the item cancels an upload (the Exit rule's X allowance).                                                                    | Cancelling a running upload is not leaving a screen; "<" would read as back.                        | 9 Nov 2026    |
| ui/PendingChip                                                       | X removes an attached file before send.                                                                                           | Removing one attachment is not an exit; Bin is for deleting saved things.                           | 9 Nov 2026    |
| Typed query                                                          | X clears a typed query.                                                                                                           | Clearing text in place is not an exit.                                                              | 9 Nov 2026    |
| Phone format row (ui/WritingBox)                                     | X closes the phone format row; X and send are matching filled circles.                                                            | The row is a mode inside the composer, not a panel over the app.                                    | 8 Nov 2026    |
| Voice recording (ui/WritingBox)                                      | X cancels a recording or removes a waiting voice note.                                                                            | Throwing a recording away is not an exit.                                                           | 9 Nov 2026    |
| Chat edges                                                           | Chat uses a 12 edge (CHAT_GUTTER), not the 16 phone / 24 laptop page gutter (L2).                                                 | Messages need the width; the written exception to L2 (ui/layout.ts).                                | 9 Nov 2026    |
